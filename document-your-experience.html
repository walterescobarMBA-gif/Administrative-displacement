Why chronology matters

Sequence can reveal what isolated documents cannot.

Housing processes often unfold across email, telephone calls, portals, appointments, notices, providers, and agencies. A chronology helps separate what happened from what is remembered, what is documented from what is disputed, and what remains unanswered.
