Before sending

Protect private information.

Email may not be appropriate for sensitive housing, health, identity, or legal records. A participant should avoid sending unnecessary confidential information and should use a secure method when one is required.

This project does not provide legal representation, emergency response, or case-management services. For an urgent housing, safety, or legal deadline, use an appropriate qualified local service.
